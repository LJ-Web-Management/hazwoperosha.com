40 vs 24 vs 8 Hour HAZWOPER: Which Training Do You Need?

  4. 8 hour refresher
  5. 8 hour supervisor
  6. Quick decision table

The number of hours of HAZWOPER training you need depends on two things: what you do on site and how much exposure you may have. Here is how OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120(e)(3) sorts workers.

40 hour HAZWOPER

Required for general site workers at uncontrolled hazardous waste sites who are exposed, or may be exposed, to hazardous substances at or above permissible exposure limits, or who need respirators. Typical roles: laborers, heavy equipment operators, drillers, remediation technicians, sampling crews and environmental field staff.

  • 40 hours of instruction off the site
  • Plus at least 3 days of actual field experience under a trained, experienced supervisor

Industry versions cover the same 40 hours with scenarios from your field: construction, environmental remediation, oil and gas spill response, fire and HAZMAT teams and utility and pipeline. It is also offered in Spanish.

24 hour HAZWOPER

Required for two groups:

  • Occasional site workers on site for a specific, limited task (for example, groundwater monitoring, land surveying or geophysical surveying) who are unlikely to be exposed above permissible exposure limits
  • Workers regularly on site in areas that are monitored and fully characterized, where exposures are below permissible limits and respirators are not needed, and the characterization shows no health hazards or possibility of an emergency

Plus at least 1 day of field experience under a trained, experienced supervisor. Workers at RCRA TSD facilities also need 24 hours under 1910.120(p).

Upgrading from 24 to 40 hours

A 24-hour worker who becomes a general site worker, or who has to wear a respirator, needs the additional 16 hours and 2 days of training to reach the 40-hour level (1910.120(e)(3)(iv)). Many people take the full 40 hour course so they are qualified for any site role from the start.

8 hour refresher

Everyone trained at the 40 or 24 hour level needs 8 hours of refresher training every year (1910.120(e)(8)). The 8 hour HAZWOPER refresher reviews core topics and covers relevant changes, recent incidents and lessons learned. Details: HAZWOPER refresher requirements.

8 hour supervisor

On-site managers and supervisors directly responsible for, or who supervise, employees in hazardous waste operations need the same initial training as their workers plus at least 8 additional hours on managing the employer's safety and health program, PPE program, spill containment program and health hazard monitoring (1910.120(e)(4)). Take the 8 hour supervisor course once; the annual refresher still applies, and a supervisor refresher is available.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take the 24 hour course instead of the 40 hour?

Only if your duties match 1910.120(e)(3)(ii) or (iii): occasional work for a limited task, or regular work in monitored, characterized areas below exposure limits without respirators. If you may be exposed above limits or wear a respirator, you need 40 hours.

Does 40 hour HAZWOPER include the 24 hour?

Yes. Someone trained at the 40-hour level meets the 24-hour requirement as well. Both groups then take the same 8-hour annual refresher.

Do I need the 3 days of field experience if I train online?

Yes. The field experience is separate from the 40 hours of instruction and must be completed on site under a trained, experienced supervisor, usually arranged by your employer.
