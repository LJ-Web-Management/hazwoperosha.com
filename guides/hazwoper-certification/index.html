How to Get HAZWOPER Certified Online (and How Long It Lasts)

"HAZWOPER certified" means you have completed the training OSHA requires for your role under 29 CFR 1910.120 and received a written certificate from the training provider. Here is the process from start to finish.

Step 1: Pick the right level

Most people need the 40 hour or 24 hour course. Supervisors add the 8 hour supervisor course. Workers who only need to recognize a release and call it in can take First Responder Awareness. See which HAZWOPER course do I need.

Step 2: Complete the online course

Online courses are self-paced. Log in from any device, work through the modules and knowledge checks, and pick up where you left off. Most people finish the 40-hour course in one to two working weeks.

Step 3: Pass the final exam

Each course ends with a final exam on the material. Practise first with the free HAZWOPER practice test.

Step 4: Download your certificate and wallet card

As soon as you pass, you can download a printable certificate of completion and a wallet card. These are the written certification 1910.120(e)(7) requires. Keep the PDF and send copies to your employer or client.

Step 5: Complete field experience

The 40-hour level needs 3 days, and the 24-hour level 1 day, of supervised field experience on site. This is done with your employer under a trained, experienced supervisor and recorded separately.

How long HAZWOPER certification lasts

Your initial certificate doesn't expire, but you stay qualified only by taking the 8 hour refresher every 12 months. In practice, a HAZWOPER certification is "current" for one year from your last course. See refresher requirements.

How employers verify HAZWOPER training

  • Check the certificate shows the worker's name, course title, training level, completion date and provider
  • Check the most recent refresher date is within the last 12 months
  • Confirm documentation of supervised field experience
  • Contact the training provider to confirm the certificate if in doubt

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get HAZWOPER certified?

The 40 hour course takes at least 40 hours of training time and the 24 hour course at least 24 hours. Online courses are self-paced, so you can finish as fast as your schedule allows. Field experience is extra.

Does OSHA issue HAZWOPER cards?

No. OSHA sets the training requirements but does not issue HAZWOPER certificates. The training provider issues the certificate and wallet card.

Does HAZWOPER certification expire?

The initial certificate stays valid as proof of initial training, but you must complete 8 hours of refresher training every year to remain qualified.

This guide summarizes federal OSHA, Cal/OSHA and EPA rules for training purposes. It is not legal advice. Always check the current regulation text and your site safety and health plan.
