# Cal/OSHA HAZWOPER (Title 8 CCR 5192): California Training Guide

HAZWOPER guide · 2 min read · Updated 2026-10-30

California runs its own OSHA-approved state plan, so HAZWOPER work there falls under **Cal/OSHA, Title 8 California Code of Regulations Section 5192**, "Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response." It follows the federal 29 CFR 1910.120 structure, and state plans must be at least as effective as federal OSHA.

## Who needs Cal/OSHA HAZWOPER

The same groups as federal HAZWOPER, for work in California: cleanup workers at uncontrolled hazardous waste sites, RCRA corrective action and voluntary cleanup crews, employees of hazardous waste treatment, storage and disposal facilities, and emergency responders to hazardous substance releases. Construction work on these sites is covered as well.

## California-specific points

- Cal/OSHA enforces 8 CCR 5192, and California regulations are cited instead of federal ones
- California's Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) regulates hazardous waste under Title 22 CCR, which is broader than federal RCRA and includes California-only hazardous wastes
- California also requires an Injury and Illness Prevention Program (IIPP) under 8 CCR 3203, which your HAZWOPER program should fit into
- Clients and agencies in California often ask specifically for Cal/OSHA-labelled certificates

## Frequently asked questions

### Is federal OSHA HAZWOPER accepted in California?

For work in California, training should meet Cal/OSHA 8 CCR 5192. The content closely matches federal 1910.120, but many California employers and agencies require Cal/OSHA-specific certificates.

### What is the Cal/OSHA HAZWOPER regulation?

Title 8 California Code of Regulations Section 5192, Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response.

### Does Cal/OSHA require an annual HAZWOPER refresher?

Yes. Like federal OSHA, Cal/OSHA requires 8 hours of refresher training every year for trained hazardous waste workers.
