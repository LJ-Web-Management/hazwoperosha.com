## About OSHA HAZWOPER (29 CFR 1910.120)

### What the standard covers

The Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response standard protects workers at uncontrolled hazardous waste sites, EPA Superfund and state cleanup sites, RCRA treatment, storage and disposal facilities, and anyone responding to an emergency release of a hazardous substance. Construction work on these sites falls under the identical 29 CFR 1926.65.

### What you will learn

HAZWOPER courses cover hazard recognition, toxicology, site characterization, air monitoring and direct-reading instruments, PPE levels A through D, respiratory protection, decontamination, confined space hazards, site control and work zones, the site safety and health plan, medical surveillance, spill containment and emergency response procedures.

## HAZWOPER Training FAQ

### What is HAZWOPER training?

HAZWOPER stands for Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response. It is the OSHA standard at 29 CFR 1910.120 (and 29 CFR 1926.65 for construction) that requires training for workers who clean up hazardous waste sites, work at RCRA treatment, storage and disposal (TSD) facilities, or respond to hazardous substance releases.

### Who needs 40 hour HAZWOPER training?

General site workers at uncontrolled hazardous waste sites, such as laborers, equipment operators and remediation technicians, who may be exposed to hazardous substances at or above permissible exposure limits must complete 40 hours of off-site instruction plus 3 days of supervised field experience under 29 CFR 1910.120(e)(3)(i).

### What is the difference between 40 hour and 24 hour HAZWOPER?

The 40-hour course is for general site workers with potential exposure above permissible limits. The 24-hour course is for occasional site workers, or workers in areas already monitored and characterized where exposures are below permissible limits and respirators are not required, per 29 CFR 1910.120(e)(3)(ii) and (iii).

### How often do I need a HAZWOPER refresher?

OSHA requires 8 hours of refresher training every year (29 CFR 1910.120(e)(8)) for workers who completed 40-hour or 24-hour HAZWOPER. Supervisors also take the annual refresher, and many take the 8-hour supervisor refresher.

### Is online HAZWOPER training accepted by OSHA?

Yes. Online instruction can satisfy the classroom portion of HAZWOPER training when it covers the required topics and the trainee has access to a qualified trainer for questions. Employers remain responsible for any site-specific instruction and the supervised field experience OSHA requires.
